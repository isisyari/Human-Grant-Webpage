HUMAN Course Offerings

Courses supported by the HUMAN Grant exploring artificial intelligence through humanistic inquiry.

3.1
FIYS

First Year Studies

2025-2026 Academic Year

FIYS 115

Climate Change Across Disciplines

Todd Beer

Explores climate change through social, cultural, political, economic, ethical, and psychological perspectives, including AI's growing impact on energy and potential solutions.

FIYS 116

Reimagining the Stage: AI and the Performing Arts

Bob Knuth

Explores AI as a creative tool in performing arts, examining applications in playwriting, design, marketing, and virtual reality performances.

FIYS 121

Photographic Modernism

Maggie Hazard

Histories and theories of photography from 1890-1945, interrogating AI technology relative to historic photographs including ethics of manipulation and dissemination.

FIYS 126

Becoming Human in the Age of AI

Aaron Greenberg

Explores cultural, ethical, and philosophical questions around AI through Western, Indigenous, African, Asian, and other perspectives on human identity and creativity.

FIYS 161

Narrative and Knowledge Production

Daniel Henke

Examines storytelling, identity, and the rhetorical impact of AI, exploring how advanced machines mimic and complicate narratives.

FIYS 189

Digital Dawn: Humanity, Cyberspace & AI

Justin Kee

Explores cyberspace development, AI emergence, ethical challenges, bias in datasets, and the development of AI as potential human companions.

FIYS 190

Effects of Social Media & AI

David Noskin

Examines social media use from psychological, social, and ethical perspectives, including how AI transforms platforms through algorithms and personalization.

3.2
Enhanced

Course Enhancements

Existing courses with HUMAN-supported AI components

ART 200

New Media Art and Design

Kimiko Matsumura

Examines new media at the cutting edge of art and design, exploring how technology shapes visual cultures through Postmodernism, Anthropocene, and Post-humanism.

ENTP 260

Developing, Protecting and Monetizing IP in the Age of AI

Gary Johnson

Foundations for protecting innovations and examining ethical and human challenges facing intellectual law and regulation in the age of AI.

JOUR 120

Introduction to Journalism

Pamela Ferdinand

Fundamental journalistic skills with focus on AI technology's ethics, bias, and potential impact on news production.

PHIL 222

The Humanist Ethics of AI

Justin Kee

Equips students to work effectively with AI, emphasizing ethical development, equitable advancement, and meaningful human-AI collaboration.

PHIL 283

Philosophy of Intelligence

Roshni Patel

Reflects on the nature of intelligence across plants, animals, humans, computers, and networks, examining biases in our determinations.
