Why Play Matters

These experiments aren't just fun, they demonstrate real AI concepts:

  • Pattern Recognition: Quick, Draw! shows how neural networks learn to identify visual patterns
  • Sequence Prediction: AI Duet demonstrates how AI can learn musical patterns and improvise
  • Semantic Understanding: Talk to Books uses natural language processing to understand meaning
  • Transfer Learning: Teachable Machine shows how models can be quickly trained for new tasks

Playing with AI helps demystify the technology and builds intuition for how these systems work.
