HUMAN Team

An interdisciplinary group of Lake Forest College faculty and staff ensuring the success of the grant's activities.

2.1
Leadership

Principal Investigators

RW

Rachel Whidden

HUMAN Principal Investigator & Project Director

Associate Professor of Communication. As Chair of the Academic Honesty Judicial Board, Rachel first began exploring the ethical dimensions of generative AI use in education, particularly regarding its impact on authorship and originality.

BZ

Ben Zeller

HUMAN Principal Investigator & Project Director

Professor of Religion. His research focuses on the intersection of religion and science, including religion and technology. He teaches a class on Religion, SciFi, AI, and Non-Human, exploring questions of personhood, consciousness, and the supernatural within AI.

TC

Tommy Crawford

HUMAN Book Club Convener & Steering Committee

Reference & Instruction Librarian at Donnelley and Lee Library. He serves as the AI library point person and has a longstanding interest in speculative fiction and how we explore attitudes about intelligence and embodiment through written word.

AR

Amy Roby

HUMAN Grant Manager

Joined Lake Forest College in 2021 and manages the Krebs Center for the Humanities. Prior to academia, she built a career in corporate sales, marketing, and communications. An enthusiastic mosaicist in her free time.
